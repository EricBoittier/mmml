## The live lead

The runs had **no electrostatics between any pair closer than 6 Å**:

| | |
|---|---|
| ML term | active below 6 Å, but reports `electrostatics ✗ off`, `charges False` |
| MM term | `mm_switch_on 6.0 Å`, `complementary True` → off below 6 Å |

Same in the 2D dimer evaluation and in the bulk NVT run that heated to 1446 K.
The log also shows `include_electrostatics True` next to `electrostatics ✗ off`
— those disagree, and that contradiction is the thing to trace.

A water hydrogen bond is 80–90% electrostatic, so this plausibly explains BOTH
the flat dimer surface and the bulk collapse with one fault. Not yet proven.

## Measurements that stand

- Dimer gate, 600-frame water grid (25 R × 24 θ, rigid TIP3):
  classical TIP3–TIP3 reference span **28.81** kcal/mol, min **−4.92** at
  R=2.88 Å θ=165°; hybrid span **1.354**, min **−0.052** at R=3.44 Å θ=300°.
  At the H-bonded frame 59 the hybrid is **+0.202 (repulsive)**.
  Correlation ref vs hybrid r = 0.51, slope 10.3 — so NOT a clean units bug
  (that would be slope ≈ 23.06, r ≈ 1).
- Bulk NVT: E_pot −8750.81 → −11903.94 eV, T 297.6 → 1446.2 K over 3100 steps
  = 99.3 kcal/mol per molecule released. Identical at per-step and 40-step
  neighbour rebuilds (1446 vs 1403 K), and present in NVE too.
- NVE conserves E_tot to **0.70 meV** over steps 400–1200 (per-step rebuild)
  vs 127 meV (every 40) — 180× flatter, worth adopting independently.

## Defects found and fixed (all verified numerically)

1. `jaxmd.build_parser()` registered two options twice → raised before
   returning; `md-system --backend jaxmd` could not run at all.
2. Monoatomic residues unbuildable at three layers (zero extent treated as
   degenerate) — blocked AR1/KR1/XE1 and CLA/POT/SOD/LIT.
3. Noble-gas RTF/PRM aborted CHARMM (bare `*` ends a title block).
4. Campaign never loaded its boxes: `RESI:1` is an absolute count, so runs were
   **one-molecule** systems.
5. NpT position cotangent missing boxᵀ — ratio 28.09 → 1.0071.
6. NpT virial cotangent was `None` — P collapsed to 2KE/3V (4059.58 measured vs
   4059.63 predicted). First fix was WRONG (atomic virial is invalid under MIC);
   replaced with a central difference of the real energy.
7. `find_worst_intermonomer_overlap` O(n²) Python loop re-inverting a constant
   cell 8.3M times — 42% of a profiled job, now **16.2×** faster, bit-identical.
8. `--evaluate-npz` hardcoded `doML/doMM/doML_dimer=True` → all term
   combinations returned bit-identical energies. Fixed.
