   The STAP  module makes the Statistical torsion angle potential energy and
   the flat-bottom distance potential. The devised functions rapidly improve
    the quality of model structures. This module proposed guiding functions
	  that are expected to facilitate protein structure modelings,
such as protein structure prediction, protein design, and structure refinement.



References :
Kim, T. R., Yang, J. S., Shin, S., & Lee, J. (2013). Statistical torsion angle potential
energy functions for protein structure modeling: a bicubic interpolation approach.
Proteins: Structure, Function, and Bioinformatics, 81(7), 1156-1165.

Ryu, H., Kim, T. R., Ahn, S., Ji, S., & Lee, J. (2014). Protein NMR structures refined without NOE data.
PloS one, 9(10), e108888.



Background

The refinement of low-quality structures is a fundamental challenge in protein structure prediction.
Many studies have been conducted on protein structure refinement. The potential energy function was derived
from distance information from given protein structures and prevented structural dislocation during the refinement process.


We demonstrate that the combination of two energy potentials, STAP and the flat-bottom distance potential,
can drive the refinement of protein structure

		      Syntax of STAP commands

[SYNTAX of STAP commands]

MOD
ROTS

ASSIGN[atom-selection*8]
[FORC real] [NPOS int]
[XMIN real] [XMAX real] [XINT real]
[YMIN real] [YMAX real] [YINT real]
[POS real*(NPOS*NPOS)]




Command description.

FORC:      Force constant of STAP
NPOS:      Number of grid points on two-dimensional surfaces, such as Phi/Psi, Phi/Chi1, Psi/Chi1, Chi1/Chi2.
Phi:       Backbone dihedral angle: (*)C-N-CA-C
Psi:       Backbone dihedral angle: N-CA-C-N(*)
Chi1:      Sidechain dihedral angle: N-CA-CB-CG
Chi2:      Sidechain dihedral angle: CA-CB-CG-CD
XMIN/YMIN: Minimun position value of X/Y (degree)
XMAX/YMAX: Maximum position value of X/Y (degree)
XINT/YINT: Interval parameter of X/Y     (degree)
RESEt:     Reset the assigned energy potentials

^_

			Usage Examples and Compatibility

The examples below illustrate some of the uses of STAP and the flat-bottom distance
     potential energy and Forces Module. See c47test/stap.inp for example.


--------------------------------------
THERE ARE REQUIREMENTS TO RUN STAP
--------------------------------------
1)	Create proper stap topology file for target protein model structure


!Example 1

! Read topology
open read card unit 10 name @0/top_all22_prot.rtf
read rtf  card unit 10
clos unit 10

! Read parameter
open read card unit 12 name @0/par_all22_prot.prm
read para card unit 12
clos unit 12

! Read in PDB files
open read card unit 12 name @0/stap.pdb
read sequ pdb unit 12
clos unit 12

! generate structure with the sequences
generate pro0 setup

! Read in PDB file and create resid
open read card unit 12 name @0/stap.pdb
read coor pdb  unit 12 resid
clos unit 12

ic para
ic build
hbuild

coor print sele .not. INIT end


! STAP setup
stream @0/stap.str
ener

! Skip all energies except STAP
skip all excl emd

! Test first for STAP before minimization
test first sele resid 58:61 end
skip excl all
energy

! STAP energy
set bmin = ?emd

! Minimization
mini sd   nstep 500
mini abnr nstep 1000

ener
skip all excl emd


! Test first for STAP after minimization
test first sele resid 58:61 end

skip excl all
set amin = ?emd

write title unit 6
* STAP energy: before: @bmin after: @amin

! Reset STAP potential
MOD
reset
END

! Get total energy
energy
